# Staff Management

## Overview

This software was designed to give the Procurement administrative team a way to reserve desks for its staff.

## Background

I developed this software alongside the [Procurement Calendar](/en/projects/calendar) during a less busy period. It provided an interface where employees could reserve a desk for the days they planned to work in the office. This became necessary after a policy change required employees to come into the office three days a week instead of two.

I was asked to create a simple application to solve this problem, which I was able to do in a few days. More features were requested later, such as simulating work schedules in less typical situations.

## Features

The software has two main features:

- Employees can reserve a desk on the weekdays they choose. Division managers can change those choices or reserve desks for their team.
- Division managers can generate schedules using specific parameters, such as whether the manager is in the office that day, the number of people in the division, and each person's preferred weekdays.
